Help

Start and stop

Choose a subject on Now. Or press ⌘ Space, type time and the subject, and press Return. Type time alone to stop.

timeStop, or start the last subject
time calculusStart or switch to a subject, by name, code or keyword
time updateInstall the newest version

Pomodoro

Tick Pomodoro when you start. After the work period a tomato fills the screen: Return takes the break, Esc snoozes, Keep working skips it. The break lasts until you press I'm back.

Break menu

On a break, Tab opens the menu: Spotify, a note to Reminders, and Messages. Turn each one on in Settings.

Friends

Add a friend by their username. Once they accept, you see where each other is in the pomodoro and can write. Messages arrive at the next break, never during work.

Your data

Everything is stored as text files in ~/.timetrack, and backed up before every update. Nothing leaves your Mac, except Messages, which are encrypted.

Remove

Run uninstall.sh from the folder you installed from. Your data stays.
