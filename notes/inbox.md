# Inbox

Dump notes here in whatever form they arrive. One heading per note. No format
is required. If a note is really a rehaul rather than a change, say so in it;
the answer to a rehaul is a plan first.

Claude works from the top, one note at a time, on its own branch, against the
scratch install. A note that has been done and reported is removed from here;
its commit is the record.
