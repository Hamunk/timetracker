# Inbox

Dump notes here in whatever form they arrive. One heading per note. No format
is required — half-sentences are fine, and so is a paragraph of grumbling. If
a note is really a rehaul rather than a change, say so in it; the answer to a
rehaul is a plan first, and it helps to know that before the work starts.

Claude works from the top, one note at a time, on its own branch, against the
scratch install. It does not remove notes — strike them out or delete them
yourself once you are satisfied.
